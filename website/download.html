Android

Installing the APK

P-Sphere is not on the Google Play Store yet. You'll install it by sideloading — this takes about two minutes.

Sideloading is safe and reversible. You can remove P-Sphere at any time from your device's app settings like any other app.
1

Download the APK to your Android device

Open the download link on your phone, or transfer the psphere-v0.1.0-alpha.apk file from your PC via USB or Bluetooth.

Get the latest release →
2

Allow installation from unknown sources

Go to Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps. Find your browser or Files app and enable "Allow from this source."

The exact path varies by manufacturer. On Samsung: Settings → Biometrics and security → Install unknown apps.
3

Tap the APK file to install

Open the APK from your file manager or browser downloads. Tap Install when Android prompts you. You can re-disable "Install unknown apps" afterwards.

4

Open P-Sphere and grant permissions

Open the app and grant Photos / Media access when prompted. This is required for the app to read your camera roll. Notification permission is optional.

5

You're ready

P-Sphere will start working in the background. Open the app to see your photos, trigger a manual sync, or discover nearby devices.
