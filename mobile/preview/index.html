What changed in 1.1

  • Colour
    A full blue theme, light and dark.

    Every Material colour role is set, so nothing falls back to the default purple. Success and warning get their own green and amber.

  • Tabs
    Albums and Search only appear with a server.

    In nearby-sync mode there are three tabs. Connect a server in Settings and the other two appear.

  • Sync
    One status card with the button inside it.

    It turns green when done, red on failure, and lists per-phone problems after a partial sync.

  • Folder
    Nothing is shared until you choose a folder.

    The Sync screen shows an amber card that goes straight to Settings.

  • Account
    Signing out keeps nearby sync running.

    It no longer drops you back on the setup screen or stops background backup.

  • Tablet
    Side rail at 600dp and wider.

    Content is capped at 640dp and photo grids add columns.

  • Not here
    This page doesn't show

    The launcher icon, Android's permission prompts, the share sheet, or real network errors. Those only happen on a device.
